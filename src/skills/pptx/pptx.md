# PowerPoint Presentation Skill

## Overview
Generates executive-grade, widescreen 16:9 PowerPoint (.pptx) presentation slide decks with native shapes, KPI grids, cards, bullet structures, and comparison layouts.

## Themes (10 Visual Styles)
1. **modern_minimal** (Default): Clean, sleek, contemporary monochrome.
2. **tech_indigo**: Deep indigo with cyan and mint tech accents.
3. **corporate_blue**: Classic enterprise corporate navy and blue.
4. **dark_slate**: Modern dark slate with neon sky blue and emerald highlights.
5. **emerald_growth**: Forest and emerald green for sustainability, finance, and growth.
6. **crimson_bold**: Deep crimson and rose for high-impact executive pitches.
7. **sunset_amber**: Warm amber and burnt orange for creative and marketing decks.
8. **cyberpunk**: Neon magenta, cyan, and deep violet futuristic dark theme.
9. **academic_navy**: Traditional academic navy and steel blue.
10. **purple_luxury**: Royal purple and gold accents for premium brands.

## Structure Standards
- **Slide Dimension:** 16:9 Widescreen layout (13.33" x 7.5").
- **Slide Types:** Title, Pillar Cards, KPI Metrics Grid, Structured Bullet List, Comparison Table, Two-Column Analysis.
- **Strict Content Integrity:** Slides focus 100% on the core domain subject matter without meta prompt regurgitation.
